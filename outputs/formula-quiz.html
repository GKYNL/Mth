Fast Decision Order

  1. Ask what type of variable you have: numerical means, proportions, categories, or paired x-y data.
  2. Ask whether there are two independent groups, the same subjects twice, or one table/distribution.
  3. Use the wording to choose the tail: "different" means two-tailed; "higher/lower" means one-tailed.
  4. Only after the test type is clear, write the formula and compute.

High-Yield Formula Map

Two independent means

Two separate groups, numerical variable.

z or t = [(x̄₁ - x̄₂) - 0] / √(s₁²/n₁ + s₂²/n₂)

Paired samples

Same people/items measured twice.

t = (d̄ - 0) / (sd / √n), df = n - 1

Two proportions

Two rates or percentages are compared.

z = (p̂₁ - p̂₂) / √[p̂(1-p̂)(1/n₁ + 1/n₂)]

Chi-square table tests

Two-way table with categorical counts.

E = row total × column total / grand total

Goodness of fit

One categorical variable compared to claimed probabilities.

χ² = Σ(O - E)²/E, df = k - 1

Correlation and regression

Paired numerical x-y data.

r = Sxy / √(SxxSyy), b = Sxy/Sxx, a = ȳ - bx̄
